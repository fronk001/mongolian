Mockups v4 — the plan, clickable

Ten phone screens (375px) for the final plan of 12 Oct 2026, in the order Fred meets them: Today, a lesson, the lesson's close, Progress and Settings. They are live: answers grade, blocks move, the reader glosses on tap, and every verdict plays the plan's own sounds. Mongolian is copied exactly from words.json and sentences.json; no new Mongolian is written. XP, dates, intervals and counts are examples. Every screen uses the plan's sentence-case labels, which is itself a proposed INSTRUMENT amendment, and each screen names the other amendments it relies on. Under each phone, a live count of its chrome words is set against the plan's budget. Mongolian and the English being taught are content, so they are not counted.
